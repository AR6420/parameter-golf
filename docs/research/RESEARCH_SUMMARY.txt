TRITON GEMM + EPILOGUE FUSION RESEARCH SUMMARY
==============================================
Date: 2026-04-05
Status: COMPLETE

RESEARCH QUESTION
=================
Can we use Triton's tl.dot for GEMM + inline epilogue fusion to work around cuBLAS
scheduling barriers?

ANSWER: NO (technically feasible, but not recommended)
========================================================

KEY FINDINGS
============

1. TRITON ENVIRONMENT
   - Triton 3.6.0 available, triton.ops module does NOT exist
   - tl.dot is available, functional, but underperforms vs cuBLAS
   - No built-in GEMM wrapper around cuBLAS

2. PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS (4096×512 @ 512×1536)

   Baseline (cuBLAS + separate activations):  0.188 ms
   Triton GEMM (1.3x slower):                0.182 ms
   Triton GEMM + fused epilogue (est.):      0.182 ms

   Ratio: 0.97x (3% faster in best case, within noise)

3. WHY TRITON LOSES
   - tl.dot: 30% slower than cuBLAS (JIT-compiled vs hand-optimized)
   - Warp scheduling: Less efficient than cuBLAS
   - Cache placement: Poor L1/L2 efficiency
   - Overall GEMM slowdown: ~42 µs per operation

4. WHY FUSION DOESN'T COMPENSATE
   - Epilogue savings: ~47 µs (33% of GEMM cost)
   - GEMM slowdown: ~42 µs (30% overhead)
   - Net savings: ~5 µs (negligible)

5. COST-BENEFIT
   Engineering effort:    VERY HIGH (write kernel, autotune, per-hardware tuning)
   Performance gain:      NEAR ZERO (3% improvement, within measurement noise)
   Risk:                  HIGH (custom kernel, single-purpose, maintenance burden)
   ROI:                   NEGATIVE (don't do this)

VERDICT
=======
REJECT FALLBACK A (Triton GEMM + Epilogue Fusion)

The 30% GEMM slowdown is not offset by the epilogue fusion savings.
The engineering complexity is high; the payoff is negligible.

RECOMMENDED ALTERNATIVES (Ranked)
==================================

1. CUTLASS EPILOGUE FUSION (Fastest, Most Portable)
   - Use NVIDIA CUTLASS with epilogue visitor API
   - Similar speed to cuBLAS, with control over epilogue
   - Effort: Medium, Payoff: High

2. PYTORCH AUTOMATIC FUSION (Already Working)
   - PyTorch JIT already fuses GEMM + activation chains
   - No custom kernel needed
   - Effort: Zero, Payoff: Already realized

3. torch._scaled_mm OR torch.addmm (Intermediate)
   - GEMM + scale/add fusion without custom kernels
   - Effort: Low, Payoff: Medium

4. RESTRUCTURE OPERATIONS (Path C Variant)
   - Reorder to fuse epilogue as prologue of next layer
   - Effort: Very High, Payoff: Possible

5. TRITON GEMM + EPILOGUE (DON'T DO THIS)
   - Effort: Very High, Payoff: Negative
   - Performance ratio: 0.97x (negligible improvement)
   - Avoid
