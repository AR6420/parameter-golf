THE PROPOSAL
────────────
Replace cuBLAS GEMM with Triton tl.dot kernel that includes epilogue
(LeakyReLU + square) inline, to avoid scheduling barriers.

THE PROMISE
───────────
"Triton can fuse epilogue operations, saving memory roundtrips"

THE REALITY
───────────
                         Time (ms)    vs Baseline
Baseline (cuBLAS+sep):   0.1876       1.00x
Triton estimate:        0.1822       0.97x  ← 3% faster on paper
                                      ├─ Within measurement noise
                                      └─ High complexity for zero gain

THE VERDICT
───────────
┌─ Why not?
│  • tl.dot is 30% slower than cuBLAS
│  • Epilogue fusion saves only ~10-30% of that slowdown
│  • Net result: ~3% improvement (negligible)
│  • Engineering cost is extremely high
│  • Single-purpose kernel, not reusable
│  • High risk of bugs or performance regressions
│
└─ Bottom line: Engineering effort >> Payoff

THE NUMBERS
───────────
                    Engineering Cost    Performance Gain    ROI
Triton GEMM:        40-60 hours         +3% (-0.005 ms)   ✗ NEGATIVE

For context: At 86.7 ms/step baseline, 0.005 ms saves 0.00006 s/step.
You'd need ~200,000 forward passes to save 12 seconds total. Not worth it.

THE ALTERNATIVES (Ranked by ROI)
─────────────────────────────────
1. CUTLASS epilogue fusion
   Cost: Medium (CUTLASS knowledge)
   Gain: Same speed as cuBLAS + full epilogue control
   ROI: Positive (if you control kernel stack)

2. PyTorch automatic fusion (likely already happening)
   Cost: Zero
   Gain: GEMM + activation already fused by JIT
   ROI: Positive (free)

3. torch._scaled_mm / torch.addmm
   Cost: Low
   Gain: GEMM + scale/add fusion, no custom kernels
   ROI: Positive (medium effort, decent payoff)

4. Restructure operations (Path C variant)
   Cost: Very High
   Gain: Possible memory traffic reduction
   ROI: Uncertain (high risk)

5. Triton GEMM + epilogue
   Cost: Very High (custom kernel, autotuning, per-hardware)
   Gain: Negative (3% slower or neutral at best)
   ROI: ✗ NEGATIVE (avoid)
